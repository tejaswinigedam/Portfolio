About

I'm a product designer who enjoys untangling messy, high-stakes workflows — tender management, clinical records, grading, healthcare staffing — and turning them into experiences that feel calm and obvious to use.

Much of my recent work sits at the intersection of AI and human judgment: designing systems where automation genuinely reduces effort while keeping the person informed and in control of the decisions that matter.

I've taken products from 0→1 as a founding designer and contributed to established teams — moving from research and problem validation through workflows, IA, and high-fidelity UI, working closely with PMs and engineers along the way.

What I do

  • 0→1 product design
  • UX research & workflow mapping
  • Information architecture
  • Designing for AI + human control
  • Wireframes → high-fidelity UI
  • Prototyping & dev collaboration

How I think & solve problems

01

Understand the real workflow

I start with research — users, market, competitors — and map the end-to-end process before touching a screen. The design follows the workflow, not the other way around.

02

Reduce complexity, not control

Especially with AI, I focus on where automation helps versus where the human must stay in charge. Clarity about what's done, pending, and needs attention beats raw feature count.

03

Structure information for findability

In dense, data-heavy products, hierarchy and IA do the heavy lifting. I design so the right information surfaces at the right moment without overwhelming the user.

04

Ship with the team

I iterate closely with product and engineering, pressure-testing designs against technical constraints so what ships stays true to the intent.
