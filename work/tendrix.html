The story

The Hours Behind Every Tender Submission

How I explored a manual tender-preparation workflow and turned scattered steps into a more structured process.

Problem

Tender teams spend hours reading requirements, checking eligibility and preparing the work needed for submission.

Focus

Understand where the manual effort comes from and explore ways to connect requirements, company documents and actions.

Outcome

A structured workflow designed to reduce repetitive work and make the process easier to execute.

Context

First, what is a tender?
